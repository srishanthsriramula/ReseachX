# ResearchX: The Complete Scientific Story (v1 → v24)

## Abstract
This monograph records the complete empirical and theoretical evolution of **ResearchX**: the search for zero-interference surgical capability repair in large-scale foundation models. Over 24 generations on **Laguna-XS.2 (33.4B MoE)**, we progressed from causal expert zero-ablation, through routing avalanche dynamics, stratified attention LoRA, Riemannian Fisher damping, and whitened geodesic subspaces, to the empirical falsification of activation-covariance whitening and the formulation of Fisher-gradient subspace invariance.

7. **v24 (The Gradient/Fisher Paradigm)**: Replaced activation covariance with Fisher Gradient Covariance $G = \mathbb{E}[\|\partial L/\partial y\|^2 xx^T]$ and scaled calibration support to 180 code tasks.
